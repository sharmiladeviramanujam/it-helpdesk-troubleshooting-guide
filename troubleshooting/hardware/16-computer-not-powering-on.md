# Computer Not Powering On

## 1. Problem Description
The user is unable to turn on their desktop computer or laptop. The device may show no signs of power, or it may briefly start and shut down.

## 2. Symptoms
- Computer does not turn on when pressing the power button.
- No power indicator lights are visible.
- Laptop screen remains black.
- Fans do not spin.
- Computer starts briefly and shuts down.
- Charging indicator is not visible on a laptop.

## 3. Possible Causes
- Power cable disconnected or damaged.
- Faulty power outlet or power strip.
- Laptop battery completely discharged.
- Faulty charger or adapter.
- Hardware failure.
- Overheating or internal power issues.

## 4. Diagnostic Steps
1. Check whether the power cable is securely connected.
2. Verify that the wall outlet is working using a known working device.
3. Check the power adapter and charging indicator on laptops.
4. Inspect the power cable for visible damage.
5. Disconnect unnecessary peripherals.
6. Observe whether any indicator lights, fan sounds, or startup activity occur when pressing the power button.

## 5. Solutions
1. Connect the computer directly to a working power outlet.
2. Ensure the power cable is firmly connected at both ends.
3. For laptops, connect the charger and allow the device to charge for several minutes.
4. Disconnect external devices such as USB drives, docking stations, and printers.
5. If permitted by the manufacturer, perform a power reset:
   - Disconnect the power adapter.
   - Remove the battery only if it is designed to be removable.
   - Hold the power button for approximately 15–20 seconds.
   - Reconnect power and attempt to start the computer.
6. Test with a compatible, known-working charger if available and authorized.
7. If there are signs of physical damage, burning smell, liquid exposure, or unusual heat, stop troubleshooting and escalate immediately.

## 6. Verification
- Confirm the computer powers on successfully.
- Verify that the manufacturer's logo or startup screen appears.
- Confirm that the operating system loads.
- Check whether the device remains powered on without shutting down unexpectedly.

## 7. Prevention
- Use reliable power sources and surge protection.
- Avoid damaging power cables.
- Keep laptop charging ports clean and undamaged.
- Avoid blocking ventilation.
- Report power-related issues promptly.

## 8. Escalation
Escalate to hardware support if the computer remains unresponsive after basic power checks. Provide the device model, power indicator status, symptoms, and troubleshooting steps performed. Do not open the computer or attempt internal repairs unless authorized.
