# Mouse Not Working

## 1. Problem Description
The user is unable to operate the mouse properly. The cursor may not move, clicks may not register, or the mouse may disconnect intermittently.

## 2. Symptoms
- Mouse cursor does not move.
- Left-click or right-click is unresponsive.
- Mouse movement is lagging or erratic.
- Wireless mouse disconnects frequently.
- Mouse works intermittently.

## 3. Possible Causes
- Loose USB connection.
- Low battery in wireless mouse.
- Bluetooth connectivity issues.
- Dirty mouse sensor or surface.
- Incorrect mouse settings.
- Driver problems or hardware failure.

## 4. Diagnostic Steps
1. Check whether the mouse is powered on.
2. Inspect the USB cable or wireless receiver.
3. Test another USB port.
4. Replace or recharge the battery if wireless.
5. Test the mouse on another computer, if available.
6. Check whether the touchpad works on a laptop.
7. Determine whether movement, clicking, or both are affected.

## 5. Solutions
1. Disconnect and reconnect the mouse.
2. Try a different USB port.
3. Replace the batteries or recharge the mouse.
4. Clean the mouse sensor and use it on a suitable surface.
5. Re-pair the mouse if it uses Bluetooth.
6. Restart the computer.
7. Check mouse settings and pointer speed in Windows Settings.
8. Update or reinstall the mouse driver if required and authorized.
9. Replace the mouse if hardware failure is confirmed.

## 6. Verification
- Confirm that the cursor moves smoothly.
- Test left-click, right-click, and scrolling.
- Verify that the mouse stays connected.
- Confirm normal operation after restarting the computer.

## 7. Prevention
- Keep the mouse sensor clean.
- Use a suitable mouse pad.
- Replace wireless batteries regularly.
- Avoid damaging cables or USB receivers.
- Keep device drivers updated.

## 8. Escalation
Escalate to IT support if the mouse remains unresponsive after basic troubleshooting. Include the mouse type, connection method, symptoms, and steps performed.
