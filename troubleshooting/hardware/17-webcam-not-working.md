# Webcam Not Working

## 1. Problem Description
The user is unable to use the webcam during video meetings or camera applications. The camera may not be detected, may display a black screen, or may show an error.

## 2. Symptoms
- Webcam displays a black screen.
- Camera is not detected by applications.
- Video does not appear during meetings.
- Camera error messages appear.
- Webcam works in one application but not another.

## 3. Possible Causes
- Camera permission settings are disabled.
- Webcam is being used by another application.
- Loose USB connection for external webcams.
- Outdated or corrupted camera driver.
- Camera privacy shutter is closed.
- Hardware malfunction.

## 4. Diagnostic Steps
1. Check whether the webcam is physically connected.
2. Verify that the privacy shutter is open.
3. Open the Windows Camera application and test the webcam.
4. Check camera permissions in Windows Settings.
5. Confirm that the correct camera is selected in the meeting application.
6. Close other applications that may be using the camera.
7. Test the webcam in another application.

## 5. Solutions
1. Disconnect and reconnect the external webcam.
2. Restart the computer.
3. Open Windows Settings → Privacy & security → Camera.
4. Enable camera access and allow applications to use the camera, where required.
5. Close other video conferencing or camera applications.
6. Select the correct webcam in the application's video settings.
7. Update or reinstall the camera driver through Device Manager if necessary and authorized.
8. If using an external webcam, test another USB port.

## 6. Verification
- Confirm the webcam displays video in the Camera application.
- Test the webcam in the user's meeting application.
- Verify that video and camera permissions work correctly.
- Confirm the issue is resolved after restarting the application.

## 7. Prevention
- Keep camera drivers updated.
- Check camera permissions before meetings.
- Avoid covering the camera lens or privacy shutter unintentionally.
- Disconnect external webcams safely.

## 8. Escalation
Escalate to IT support if the webcam is not detected or continues displaying errors after troubleshooting. Include the device model, application affected, error message, and troubleshooting steps performed.
